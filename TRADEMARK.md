# HomeAccounting Trademark and Brand Usage Policy

The HomeAccounting **software** is free and open source under
[AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The HomeAccounting **name, logo, and visual identity** are
not — they are trademarks of the project, and this policy explains how you may
use them.

This is normal for open-source projects and is not a restriction on your
software freedom. Its purpose is narrow: when someone sees the HomeAccounting
name on a personal-finance application, they must be able to trust that it is
this project, with this project's security posture. A fork that changes the code
and keeps the name breaks that guarantee for everyone.

**Status:** trademark registration is in progress. This policy applies to the
unregistered marks in the meantime.

## You may, without asking

- State truthfully that your project, product, or article **works with**, **is
  built on**, **integrates with**, or **is compatible with** HomeAccounting.
- Use the name in prose to refer to this project — in documentation, blog posts,
  talks, comparisons, reviews, and academic work — including critical ones.
- Run an **unmodified** build of HomeAccounting for yourself, your family, your
  company, or your clients, and call it HomeAccounting.
- Use the logo to link to the project or to indicate an integration, without
  altering its colours, proportions, or elements.
- Redistribute unmodified official builds and packages under the project name.

## You must rebrand if you

- Distribute a **modified** version of HomeAccounting. Change the name, the
  logo, and the visible branding. You keep every freedom the AGPL gives you over
  the code; you simply may not call the result HomeAccounting.
- Operate a **hosted service** based on HomeAccounting for third parties under a
  name containing "HomeAccounting", or in a way that implies it is the official
  managed service.

When you rebrand, please keep an accurate attribution such as "based on
HomeAccounting" — that is encouraged, and is not a trademark use we object to.

## You may never

- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests your project, service, or company
  is official, endorsed by, or affiliated with HomeAccounting when it is not.
- Register the name, a confusingly similar name, or the logo as your own
  trademark, company name, or domain in a way that suggests official status.
- Use the marks on merchandise for sale, or in advertising for an unrelated
  product.
- Alter the logo, or combine it with other marks in a way that creates a new
  composite mark.

## Domains, social accounts, and package names

Do not register domain names, social-media handles, or package-registry names
that contain "homeaccounting" in a way that implies you are the project.
`homeaccounting-monzo-provider` as a community package is fine;
`homeaccounting-cloud.com` as a competing hosted service is not.

## Questions and permission

This policy cannot anticipate everything, and we would rather say yes to a
reasonable use than have you guess. Ask at `legal@homeaccounting.com`, or open a
discussion. Permission granted in writing overrides anything above.

We reserve the right to revise this policy; revisions will not be applied
retroactively to uses already permitted in writing.
