# HomeAccounting Trademark and Brand Usage Policy

**This policy now lives at <https://www.homeaccounting.com/trademark>.**

It moved there because its audience is mostly people who will never open a
repository — someone naming a package, writing an article, or weighing up a
fork — and because four repositories linking one URL is better than four copies
drifting apart. See [tracker#8].

This file is kept as a pointer so existing links and forked checkouts do not
dead-end. The short version, unchanged:

- The **software** is free under [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **name, logo and
  visual identity** are not.
- Truthful "works with" use, and running an **unmodified** build, need no
  permission.
- Distribute a **modified** build, or operate a hosted service for third
  parties, and you must **rebrand** — you keep every AGPL freedom over the
  code, you simply may not call the result HomeAccounting.
- The marks are **unregistered**; no application is on file.

Questions: <legal@homeaccounting.com>.
