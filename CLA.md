## How to sign

You do not need to send anything by email or post. When you open your first pull
request, a bot will comment on it asking you to reply with a single line:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your signature — GitHub username, and the pull request in which you signed — is
recorded in this repository. You will not be asked again for later pull
requests.
